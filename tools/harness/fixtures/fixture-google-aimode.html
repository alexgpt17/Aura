what is ashwagandha

AI Overview

Ashwagandha (Withania somnifera) is an ancient evergreen shrub native to parts of India, Africa, and the Middle East that has been used for thousands of years in traditional Ayurvedic medicine.

It is classified as an adaptogen, a plant substance thought to help the body manage and resist physical and mental stress. Its active compounds, primarily withanolides, are believed to interact with stress-response systems like the hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal axis and lower cortisol.

Common uses include stress and anxiety, sleep, and exercise performance.
