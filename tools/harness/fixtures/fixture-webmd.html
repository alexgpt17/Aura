Alpha-GPC - Uses, Side Effects, and More

Written by Chelsey McIntyre, PharmD; reviewed by Beth Johnston, BCPS, PharmD

Common/Generic Name(s): alpha-GPC (a-GPC), choline alfoscerate

Classes: Nootropics

Overview

Alpha-GPC is a chemical that releases choline in the brain. It is a prescription drug in some countries and a dietary supplement in the US.

Featured: Brain health

Text set over a photograph.

People use alpha-GPC for memory, athletic performance and other purposes, but there is no good scientific evidence to support most of these uses.
