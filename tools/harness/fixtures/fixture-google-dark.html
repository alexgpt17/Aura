what is ashwagandha

AI Overview

Ashwagandha (Withania somnifera) is a small evergreen shrub belonging to the nightshade family, native to parts of India, Africa, and the Middle East. For thousands of years, it has been a staple in traditional Ayurvedic medicine, where it is often referred to as "Indian ginseng".

It is classified as an adaptogen, a natural substance believed to help the body manage and resist physical and mental stress. Its active compounds, primarily withanolides, are believed to interact with stress-response systems.

Common uses include stress and anxiety, sleep, and exercise performance.
