# Aura Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** July 25, 2026

Aura (“the App”) is developed by Alex Martens. This policy explains what information the App handles and how it is used.

## Summary

Aura does not collect, sell, or transmit personal data to any remote server. Theme preferences and related settings stay on your device (and in the shared App Group used by Aura’s Safari extensions).

## Data stored on your device

Aura may store the following locally:

- Theme colours and custom themes you create
- Per-website theme overrides (hostnames you configure)
- Content blocker category preferences
- App appearance preferences (accent colour, light/dark mode)
- Onboarding completion state

This data is stored using iOS App Group UserDefaults shared between the Aura app and its Safari extensions so themes can apply in Safari. It is not uploaded to our servers — Aura has no backend.

## Safari Web Extension

The Safari Web Extension requests access to websites so it can apply your chosen colour themes. It reads page structure only as needed to recolor content on your device. Page contents are not sent off-device.

The Content Blocker extension uses bundled local blocklists to hide ads, trackers, social widgets, and annoyances according to the categories you enable. Blocklists are not fetched from the network at runtime.

## Permissions

- **Safari Extensions:** Required for theming and optional content blocking. You enable these in iOS Settings → Safari → Extensions.
- Aura does not use location services, tracking (ATT), advertising identifiers, or analytics SDKs.

## Children’s privacy

Aura does not knowingly collect data from children. The App does not create accounts.
