Aura Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

Aura (“the App”) is developed by Alex Martens. This policy explains what information the App handles and how it is used.

Summary

Aura does not collect, sell, or transmit personal data to any remote server. Theme preferences and related settings stay on your device (and in the shared App Group used by Aura’s Safari extensions).

Data stored on your device

Aura may store locally: theme colours and custom themes; per-website theme overrides (hostnames you configure); content blocker category preferences; app appearance preferences; and onboarding completion state. This data is stored using iOS App Group UserDefaults shared between the Aura app and its Safari extensions. It is not uploaded to our servers — Aura has no backend.

Safari Web Extension

The Safari Web Extension requests access to websites so it can apply your chosen colour themes. It reads page structure only as needed to recolor content on your device. Page contents are not sent off-device. The Content Blocker extension uses bundled local blocklists according to categories you enable. Blocklists are not fetched from the network at runtime.

Permissions

Safari Extensions are required for theming and optional content blocking. You enable these in iOS Settings → Safari → Extensions. Aura does not use location services, tracking (ATT), advertising identifiers, or analytics SDKs.
